# Chip audit against the written spine

Source: `docs/original-story-spine.md` (Brian's Nov 2 bible), the Chapter 2 kitchen revision in `docs/story-bible.md`, `docs/STORY-DECISIONS.md`, and the build on `phase-2-structure`.

Method (Brian): one seven-beat spine for the whole game, each chapter as its own seven beats one level down, nothing finer. Every chip's scene is placed on a beat at both levels. A chip fails only if its scene belongs to no beat, or its goal pulls against the beat it sits in. Chip wording is not scored on the armature; a chore chip is fine. The spine is held lightly: where the build and the spine differ, the aim is the better story, and each departure below carries a verdict.

Beats: 1 Once upon a time, 2 Every day, 3 Until one day, 4 Because of that, 5 Because of that, 6 Until finally, 7 Ever since.

Marks: **B** = Brian wrote this label in the spine. **R** = my reading, where he gave none. Chips audited: 84 (Phase 1 rows 5.1 to 5.21; Phase 2 rows 18.1 to 18.62 and 18.144).

## Structural gaps (the main finding)

These hold whichever version of each scene Brian keeps. Ranked.

1. **The beat 5 to beat 6 chain depends on a scene the build lacks.** M4 asks "Then who were the guardians?" and M5 pays it off, but Chapter 2 never shows the player the answer (departure 1). In the build the question is false (Squatch was told "Mops signed it") and the player has nothing to wait for. Fix in either version: show the player, not Squatch.
2. **Game beat 5 carries three chapters.** Chapter 2, all of Chapter 3 and Chapter 4 M1 to M4 sit inside one "Because of that", and "Until finally" is the last scene only (M5). Between Chapter 1's four beats and Chapter 4's single climax, the middle has no marked turn. Candidate turn: Chapter 3's loss (Mops floats away, "I'll always come get you") as the end of beat 5, with Chapter 4 as beat 6 start to finish. Brian's call, and it moves the Chapter 4 chips from game beat 5 to 6.
3. **Beat 2 (Every day) is empty at chapter level in Chapters 2 to 4.** Ch2 G1 is a rule ("not without coins"), not a routine; Ch3 has one line ("Pretzels to Mars every day"); Ch4 opens on a sneak. The spine has none either, so the build matches it. A rule-of-the-place beat is what makes the next "Until one day" land.
4. **Chapter 2 has the same job twice.** wash1 and wash5 are both "wash a set of dishes for a jar of coins"; the soup between them is the only turn. Beat 6 holds three phases (second wash, pay, bots and ship).
5. **Chapter 1 carries four of seven game beats (about 20 scenes), and its beat 7 is snacks and a gauge.** The weight is fine for setup, but a 10-year-old has seen most of the spine before the first gas stop.
6. **Chapter 3 beat 5 is one scene and one exit chip.** The bond talk ("still our song?") carries the whole turn.
7. **Chapter 4 beat 7 is a card.** The spine gives it one sentence; the build expands it into a told caption (departure 6), where a drawn closing image would show it.

## Where the build departs from the written spine, ranked, with a verdict

The spine is held lightly: the game has moved on since Nov 2, and the aim is the better story. Each verdict says which version has clearer cause and effect, a stronger turn and all seven beats, and is easier for a 10-year-old. Verdicts: **keep the build**, **return to the spine**, **Brian's call** (two genuinely different stories). Type: moved, missing, added, changed.

1. **Missing: Ch2 G4 flashback (FB3), and Squatch is told early.** Spine: "Years ago..." shows Mops writing the Guardians note, so the player knows and Squatch does not. Build: no flashback, and Squatch says "A hoof stamp. Mops signed it."
   **Return to the spine.** Irony gives beat 5 a promise the player waits on and makes M4's question land; the build tells Squatch, so M4 and M5 pay off a secret nobody holds.
2. **Changed: Ch2 goal.** Spine: Rustbucket would do anything for chili, Mops serves it, Rustbucket naps, Mops siphons the gas. Build: Mops earns coins, buys Squatch soup instead of gas (the bible), pays Rusty.
   **Brian's call.** The build has the stronger turn (Mops chooses Squatch's hunger over the tank) and a simple cause chain; the spine has a character want and a gag. Chips 5.4 and 5.15 to 5.17 follow whichever is kept.
3. **Changed: Ch1 O3 and O6 heart lines.** Spine: "Everybody has somebody", then "You have me." Build: "Robots?" and "I wanted to see the robots."
   **Return to the spine.** Loneliness is why he breaks the rule and "You have me" is what S6's third ask hurts against; curiosity about robots is a weaker cause for beat 2 and for the fight.
4. **Changed: Ch1 O4 word.** Spine: FUR words only, "Wookie" not until O7. Build: "A WOOKIE!" from the kid, exec and demo bot.
   **Return to the spine.** The O7 flashback explains the word, so saying it first shows an effect before its cause (review rules 13 and 20).
5. **Changed: Ch1 O8 gift.** Spine: a Moon Pie and a note. Build: a toy rocket and a note.
   **Return to the spine, with departure 1.** The Moon Pie links O8, the S1 stash and FB3 as a fair clue; the rocket is charming but connects to nothing. If FB3 is cut, keep the build.
6. **Changed: Ch4 M2 to M5.** Four sub-changes.
   - Ship to van: **return to the spine.** The order came on the delivery ship, and a van has no setup (rule 7).
   - Dad's "Health rules" for "You'll get hair in the pancakes": **return to the spine.** The hair joke calls back to S2's "Paperwork".
   - "You were my Guardian the whole time" for "my family": **keep the build.** It names the secret and closes the Guardians thread. It needs departure 1 to work.
   - Added "Brave Sasquatches don't hide. I was wrong" and the long Ever since card: **keep the build** for the line (Mops reverses his own rule, a real turn), **Brian's call** on the card, which tells where a drawn image could show.
7. **Missing: Ch2 G2 Blorp pea gag.** Spine: "1 pea, since 1997", Blorp weeps. Build: "Don't rush me."
   **Brian's call.** A pure gag; it neither helps nor hurts the beats. Already held in STORY-DECISIONS item 4.
8. **Added and moved: Ch1 S7 slip peeks, and the receipt line.** Build: Squatch peeks at the halves in S7 and says "The hangar code's on it..." before Mops names it in S8. Spine: the code first appears in S8.
   **Return to the spine** for the peeks (Mops's S8 line should be the reveal; rule 20). **Keep the build** for the receipt line ("Where secrets go"), which ties the order to the Guardian Box.
9. **Added: scenes and lines the spine lacks.** Chapter cards, Mops's Ch2 opener, Ch4 M0 "Home doesn't mean safe", S2's GANITOR definition, the S1 schedule panel, the LOCKUP card, Gloop's "Bots! Go watch the dock.", the S11 wardrobe in Storage.
   **Keep the build** for the cards, openers and GANITOR line (they orient a 10-year-old). **Brian's call** on "Bots! Go watch the dock." (STORY-DECISIONS item 1).
10. **Not a departure: beat 2 is empty in Chapters 2 to 4.** See structural gap 3.

## Story conflicts for Brian, checked against the written spine

1. **Cooking and asking Gloop have no chip.** The spine has Mops order the chili and take the dish job; it has no cooking at all. The bible has Mops cook the tickets. The chip table has neither COOK nor ASK · Gloop, and 5.13 tells Squatch to wash while Mops "cooks". Bible and chips agree; the spine is silent, so this stands as a gap to fill, not a clash.
2. **TV hide.** The spine settles part of STORY-DECISIONS item 1: "The diner TV shows WOOKIE SPOTTED IN SPACE! Squatch hides in a crate." So the broadcast and the crate hide are Brian's. Chips 5.6 and 5.7 fit. Still open: the bots' "You watch the crate" lines and the timed hide.
3. **Guardians.** STORY-DECISIONS item 5 says no later scene names who the Guardians are. The spine says the reveal is the plot: FB3 in Ch2, "Then who were the guardians?" in M4, a bearded goat drawn into MY GARDIANS. The build has M4 and M5 but not FB3 (departure 1). Item 5 should be rewritten and FB3 needs Brian's yes to build.
4. **Harry dance.** The spine has "Dance party, with Mops going Baaa on the beat", so a dance belongs in M5. Chip 18.62 fits. Open: whether the four-press dance is the whole party or a stub, and whether Action sings in the open as the spine says.
5. **Zip's songs.** The spine's Chapter 3 is lure puzzles ("echo singing puzzle", "Next lure prompt"), not a song set. Chips 18.40 and 18.41 fit. STORY-DECISIONS item 6 can be closed on that reading.
6. **New: the Rusty payoff (departure 2).** Chips 5.15 to 5.17 ("grab the new tip jar", "take the coins out", "give the coins to Rusty") play a different ending to Chapter 2 than the spine's chili and nap. Brian's call: keep the coins, or restore the chili for Rustbucket.
7. **New: ship or van.** The spine has Mops cleaning "the delivery ship" and Squatch saying "Go wait in the ship." The build, and chips 18.56 to 18.60, say van.
