# Story clarity pass

Proposal only. Nothing in this file is in the game yet. No scene, line, timer, or difficulty has been changed. Brian says yes to any new or changed words in `docs/lines/STORY-CLARITY-LINES.md`. Until that yes, the build stays as it is.

Read this as a 6-to-8-year-old would hear the game out loud. The order below is the order the player meets each scene in `docs/SCRIPT-AS-BUILT.md` and in `src/game/`.

**Counts**

| Kind | How many | What counts |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Reorders | 4 | An existing beat plays at a different moment |
| Cuts | 9 | A line the player can see now goes away, or stops repeating |
| Line changes | 64 | A new line, or a new wording of a line that stays |

The 64 and the 9 are the rows in the lines file. The four reorders are:

1. The TV (O7) moves to just after the peek, before the notice.
2. The welcome banner moves off the peek and onto the lobby wall (O4).
3. Zip says "Hot pretzels!" on screen before Mops explains him.
4. The cook is drawn at the stove before she yells "A WOOKIE!"

**Spelling that stays.** The box lid says `MY GARDIANS` and `MY GARDIAN`. That is the kid's spelling. Do not correct it.

**Brian's answers used here.** The Mars ride is the delivery ship. Dad's joke is hair in the pancakes. The ending is a picture. The bots stop arguing after "Bots! Go watch the dock." Zip asks a riddle that changes every game. Chapter 2 shows Mops writing the Guardians note, and Squatch does not know. Trips between chapters get a ship window, a card, and a skippable game. A dirty dish makes Gloop mad, and it is not a fail.

## Chapter 1 · Stormtop

### C1-1. The peek does not show friends

**Where.** O3, the door-crack picture.

**What a kid would be confused by.** Squatch says everyone has somebody, but the picture does not show that. Through the crack today: a blue banner, spinning confetti, a man in a grey suit standing alone, a person with a clipboard standing alone, and three tourists standing apart. Nobody hugs. Nobody looks at a friend. There are no robots. "Ha ha!" puffs happen in the closet, under the door, before the picture. A kid cannot see why he would be jealous.

**Proposed fix.** A visual beat, before any line. He already pressed PEEK. The picture holds with no speech long enough to see three groups, left to right.

- A family hug. A grown-up and a child with arms around each other, and one more person with an arm around both. Faces close. They look at each other, not at the door.
- Two tourists facing each other, leaning in, laughing. Mouths open. Little "ha ha!" puffs. The game already draws those puffs.
- Two small round robots, the same size, high-fiving. Hands meet, a small star pops, hands drop, they do it again. They are glad to have each other.

Then Squatch, already drawn at the bottom of the crack, stops smiling. His shoulders drop. He pulls his head back. The crack closes. That is the duck back in.

**Art and animation.** The hug can be one pose, or a single squeeze. The laugh is a mouth change plus the puff. The high-five is a short loop. Squatch's face is the last change, then the panel closes on its own. No new button. The three pairs are big. They are the picture, not tiny people under a sign.

**Priority.** High.

### C1-2. The peek line

**Where.** O3. Now: "...Everybody has somebody."

**What a kid would be confused by.** "Somebody" does not mean "a friend" to a six-year-old. The sentence does not sound like jealousy even after a clear picture.

**Proposed fix.** Reword. Do not cut it. The picture plays first. Then one line: "Even they have a friend." "They" is the robot pair, the last group he looked at. The line does not say the word robot. The notice is still the first time that word is read.

The line stays because O6 needs it. Mops answers "You have me." A quiet face is easy to miss on a phone. If Brian would rather have no words on the peek, say "cut" on line L3. Then O6 is the first time Squatch says he is lonely, and L6 stays as it is now.

**Priority.** High.

### C1-4. The TV explains hiding too late

**Proposed fix.** Reorder. Right after he ducks back and says "Even they have a friend.", the TV turns on by itself. Same flashback: Wookies visited, they shed, fur clogged the air machines, people gasped, "NO FUR." Cut the "WATCH · TV" button (L39) and the chip "Squatch: watch the TV" (L40). The TV is already in the closet. The zap the game already uses when the set comes on is enough.

After the card "Now", one new line (L4): "They'd call me a Wookie." Then the stay-in sign pulses, he says "Sorry, sign." (same words, later moment), and he sneaks out. The old idle line "Just one peek. Mops won't know." is wrong, because the peek already happened. It becomes "Just one step out. Mops won't know." (L5).

**Priority.** High.
