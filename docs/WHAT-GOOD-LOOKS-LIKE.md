# What good looks like (Brian's words)

Brian's own words about what Space Snack Quest should be, grouped by topic, taken from the Grok threads (Squatchbot, Grok Bot, G Bot) and the Nov 8 playtest recording. **Brian: correct anything wrong or out of date.** Once you have, this page wins over older docs.

How to read it: quotes are word for word, typos kept. Tags say where each is from: `[G#516]` G Bot thread (your most recent thinking), `[S#307]` Squatchbot thread, `[K#102]` Grok Bot thread, `[Nov 8 @12:34]` the Nov 8 recording (machine transcript, so words can be off). Where you changed your mind, only the later word is kept, with the one it replaced noted. Drafted 2026-11-22 by Claude; full threads kept privately on the Mac mini (see `docs/grok-archive/README.md`).

## Confirmed by Brian, 2026-11-22 (multiple choice)

These win over anything below that disagrees. Details in `docs/DECISIONS.md`.

- Morning: your latest order (speaker wakes him, Mops mopping, beard cutaway, song, sing along, Mops leaves for work).
- Controls: stick, tap to walk, and one fixed-size action button.
- Plant eyes: copy the very original approved eyes.
- Optional things have no outline; kids discover them. Only what you must do is outlined.
- Balloon and dance: keep, in the simplest form, shown to you before it lands.
- Dress-up: drag pieces onto a close-up of Squatch on a white background, with the disguise meter.
- Wookiee TV: full-screen story pictures.
- Sight cones: on everyone, dashed then yellow; guests "?" then "!" if he lingers; guards and robots "!" at once; Mops hearts. First a still of the party with every cone drawn, for you to look at once.

## How it should feel

- Clean and simple: "the important thing is that it's clean and simple." [G#516]

- Characters sized to each other: "We want to make sure that all the characters are relatively sized to each other." [K#138]
- A room stays the same room: "We need to have a persistent room feel for anything where it actually is the room." [S#985]
- The early look: "Yeah more of the earlier A E S T H E T I C." [S#476]
