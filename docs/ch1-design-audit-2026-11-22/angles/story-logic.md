## 1. "Caught" means three different things, and the party endings and the points check pick different ones
- **What we seem to assume:** "caught" is one idea: the party ending "Caught (alarm, run home, guard search...)" and the points rule "never caught" line up.
- **Why it may be wrong:** DECISIONS section 10, entry 2026-11-07, Brian: "Oh, it's the difference between being spotted versus being caught." Only a catch clears "Never caught! +250". Brian on guards: "if they catch him, that's when they get jailed, and we absolutely want that to be the case." [S#1126]. But today's party ending (A) calls the whole alarm path "Caught", and beat 18 says a hidden Squatch makes the guard "give up and leave", which is spotted but not caught. So a kid who is spotted, runs home and hides from the guard is in the "caught" ending, yet by Brian's Nov 7 meaning is not caught.
- **Effect if wrong:** Part 1's clean check (and "PERFECT CHAPTER!") either fires for a kid who triggered the FUR alarm, or is denied to a kid the guard never touched. This is the exact thing his kid "loves to play... perfectly".
- **Question for Brian:** For the Day 1 "clean" check, does being seen and setting off the FUR alarm already make it not clean (A, recommended: matches the two endings), or only being reached by a guard and jailed (B)?

## 2. The never-caught path has no cause for "Everyone out there has someone."
- **What we seem to assume:** both endings meet at home and the same two lines work.
- **Why it may be wrong:** Brian on this line earlier: "it doesn't make sense to me that's a time that he's Sad and scared." [S#84]. Walkthrough Open question 2 moved it to the homecoming because there Squatch has just been chased and Mops has found the flyer and fur. On the closing-time path he danced, sang and snacked unseen, and there is no fur (nothing shed it), and the flyer is optional free-play loot (beat 8). Mops finding "the flyer and fur" (beat 19, decision 8) cannot happen as written. Nothing makes him sad.
- **Effect if wrong:** the sweetest scene in the chapter lands flat for the kid who played perfectly, the best player gets the weakest emotional beat.
- **Question for Brian:** On the closing-time path, what makes him feel it: (A, recommended) the guests leave in pairs and families and he is the only one left standing alone in the empty hall when Mops finds him; (B) change his line on this path only; (C) keep the line with no setup.

## 3. The Guardians' note (beat 22) is wrong for the never-caught path, and part replay makes it worse
- **What we seem to assume:** the line is only a wording tweak "when built" (DECISIONS party ending).
- **Why it may be wrong:** beat 22 is headed "the morning after the party scare" and Squatch says "The Guardians came! They always come when I'm scared." On the clean path he was never scared. Also today's points decision lets the kid replay Day 2 alone from the chapter screen ("any single part can be replayed"), so beat 22 opens a part that must make sense with no Day 1 behind it.
- **Effect if wrong:** a perfect kid hears a line that contradicts what just happened; a replayed Part 2 refers to a scare that never occurred.
- **Question for Brian:** Which wording for beat 22 (still pending your yes): (A, recommended) one line for every path, "The Guardians came!" with no "scared" reason; (B) two versions chosen by how Day 1 ended; (C) "They always come when I need them."

## 4. What triggers "closing time" is not defined, and the old timer still sits in the same slot
- **What we seem to assume:** a never-caught party "ends at closing time" by itself.
- **Why it may be wrong:** DECISIONS: "closing time: the speaker announces it, guests leave, Mops finds him while cleaning". Nothing says when. Beat 16 still says the disguise comes off "after about 3 minutes" and Open question 4 recommends the balloon or octopus as "the sure ending", but the balloon is out. Brian said of the old fallback "Cool hat" that it "doesn't make sense" (walkthrough Q4; also his thread near [S#365] where he asked for real causes only). If the 3-minute fallback stays, nobody reaches closing time. If it goes, the party has no end for a kid who only stands still, and a kid who walks home early at minute one has no party ending either.
- **Effect if wrong:** the perfect ending is unreachable, or an idle kid sits forever in the hall.
- **Question for Brian:** When does closing time come: (A, recommended) about 3 minutes of party time on the floor, and the fallback "Cool hat" caught-ending is deleted; (B) when the kid has done a set number of party things; (C) when the kid chooses to go home.

## 5. Where is Mops during the party, and does the alarm tell him?
- **What we seem to assume:** Mops is "at work" and simply not around; he "comes home" and discovers things (beat 19).
- **Why it may be wrong:** the party is in Mops's workplace and he is the janitor; today's decision has him cleaning that hall at closing time and gives him a hearts cone, so he can see Squatch. In the caught path a station-wide FUR alarm rings and guards run to his room; Mops finding "the flyer and fur" as news is odd if the alarm already told him. Brian asked this same question for lunch: "you need to tell me where is Mops when he finds him, because right now we're not clear on that." [S#398]. No such answer exists for Day 1.
- **Effect if wrong:** Mops either appears out of nowhere at closing, or he is visibly in the hall during the party and the kid can see his hearts cone and the "you stay in" rule gets broken in front of him.
- **Question for Brian:** During the party, where is Mops: (A, recommended) off elsewhere on the station, out of the hall until closing time, and the alarm is why he hurries home in the caught path; (B) in the hall cleaning, with his hearts cone as a hazard; (C) at home.

## 6. Mops proposes Mars in beat 45 but beat 48 says Mars is a waste of time and writes a note to talk Squatch out of it
- **What we seem to assume:** the chain 40, 45, 48 reads as one consistent Mops.
- **Why it may be wrong:** beat 40 Mops says no to Mars. Beat 45 (that night, six days on) Mops: "Remember that pancake place? Let's go find out about it." Beat 48 then says the NO letter "is why Mops thinks going to Mars is a waste of time", and he writes "We know you're thinking about going to Mars. Don't worry, we'll always be with you." Brian flagged the reason problem himself: "we have to explain why he dismisses going to mars if he's been searching for parents" [S#586], and asked for that note [S#408]. The beat that explains why Mops now says yes is missing; a note that soothes a boy Mops is about to take to Mars reads as the opposite of what he is doing.
- **Effect if wrong:** the kid sees Mops leading the trip and quietly discouraging it in the same ten minutes.
- **Question for Brian:** Why does Mops now go: (A, recommended) being replaced leaves him nothing to stay for, and the pancake place is a fresh lead he trusts more than the NO letter; (B) he is going only to please Squatch and the note is meant to calm Squatch for the trip; (C) change the note's words so it is about the trip, not talking him out of it.

## 7. Mops put the paperwork in a bin, and he is the janitor
- **What we seem to assume:** the stuck-together pink and yellow copies stay in the bin until night one, and by escape night Mops can only look for the code in the vent.
- **Why it may be wrong:** beat 35 shows Mops spit them into the bin; beat 45, six days later, Mops says they "need the code" and the Squatch cover (beat 46) is "Didn't a copy get sucked into the vent?". Mops knows the bin; he empties bins. Brian's logic: "that's when the other copy gets sucked into the vent, and that's how Squatch knows it's in there." [S#426]. That is why Squatch knows. But Mops did not see the vent suck it (he was asleep, beats 41 to 42), so Mops has no reason yet to believe it, and would first ask where the sheets he spat out went.
- **Effect if wrong:** a sharp kid asks "why doesn't Mops just check the bin?" and "how does Squatch know about the vent?"
- **Question for Brian:** What does Mops think happened to the sheets: (A, recommended) he assumes the bin was emptied and takes Squatch's vent remark as a lucky lead because the vent is where he goes anyway; (B) Mops asks "how do you know?" and Squatch bluffs; (C) the bin shows empty on screen on escape night.
