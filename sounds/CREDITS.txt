impactPlate_light_000, impactPlate_light_002, impactGlass_light_001, impactMetal_light_000 (the snack pyramid's crash)

http://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/

(2026-11-24, "35 (A)"). The game plays them through its master volume, only when sound is on.
